# Concept #5 — "You Were Never Meant to Practise Alone"

> **Big idea / territory:** *community is the hero — the people, not the places.* Where
> concepts 1, 2 and 4 hang on a geographic spine (five cities, four countries) and concept 3
> on the host toolkit, this concept removes geography **entirely** and puts the human circle at
> the literal centre of the page. The single design idea is **a gathering**: many quiet,
> faceless human marks drawn into one ring around a calm open centre — a community you can
> step into. For guests, the reason the experiences matter is *the people you share them with*.
> For hosts, the value is *being found by a real, growing community already looking for what you
> hold*. The composition is centred on purpose: the ring of people **is** the focal moment, and
> the open middle is the space left for *you*.

---

## 1. Positioning

**The one idea:** *find your people.* ARIVELA is, before it is anything else, a community —
and you were never meant to practise alone. The flyer makes that feeling visible by drawing the
reader's eye to a single centred gathering of human marks and holding one quiet, open space at
its heart: the place you're invited to join. Everything else on the page radiates out from that
centre. Community is the subject, the structure, and the emotion all at once.

**Why community is the *centred* hero (the single design move):**

- **A centred human circle, not a centred text-stack.** The defining image on both sides is one
  ring of abstract, faceless human figures (duotone) or one centred gathering of real people
  (photo), placed dead-centre as the page's gravitational core. The Lora-italic hero sits with
  it — above or threaded through — but the *people* hold the centre. This is deliberately the
  opposite of a generic centred paragraph: the focal point is a community, drawn.
- **An open heart in the middle.** Inside the ring of figures we leave a small, calm pool of
  cream — negative space shaped like a welcome. It reads as *room for one more*. On the guest
  side it whispers "your place is here"; on the host side it becomes the space your offering
  fills. The breathing room is part of the meaning, not leftover.
- **Radiating, concentric rhythm.** Hairline concentric arcs ripple gently outward from the
  centre (think the quiet rings of a single drop in still water) — community as something that
  grows from the middle outward. This replaces the other concepts' horizon-of-cities with a
  *human* sense of scale and belonging.
- **Scale expressed through people, never place.** "A real, growing community" / "your people" /
  "an international community" — abstract scale only. The many small figures in the ring *are*
  the proof of scale; we never name, pin, or map a single location.

**Why this is distinct from concepts 1–4:**

- It is **not the worldwide-circle / place-led concept (1).** That concept's hero is the map of
  five cities and the feeling of geographic reach. Here there is **no map, no cities, no
  countries, no place pins, no geography rail at all** — the "circle" is a circle of *people*,
  and reach is felt only as a crowd of human marks, never a list of locations.
- It is **not the editorial-trust concept (2).** That one lets type and emptiness carry trust
  almost austerely. Here the warmth is in the *gathering itself* — the page is centred,
  populated, embracing. Belonging, not restraint, is the feeling.
- It is **not the toolkit/home concept (3).** No card system, no stepped shelves, no product
  rhythm. The host capabilities appear, but as four warm invitations radiating from the
  community centre, never as a feature spec.
- It is **not the off-center concept (4).** That one breaks symmetry on purpose and pushes the
  areas to the margins. This one is **resolutely centred and symmetrical** — the community sits
  at the heart of the page because it is the heart of the brand.

**The feeling we own:** *belonging.* The quiet relief of walking into a room and realising these
are your people — and the steadiness, for a host, of knowing a real community is already turning
toward you.

  > *there's a community*

  *(Optional short Lora-roman descender beneath, calmer: "let them find you." — the line that

  *(a quiet "soon" note — `SOON` in a thin tracked label, no exclamation, no hype.)*

  > create your host profile →
  > **arivela.co**  *(inline QR alongside, light-on-dark if it sits on an olive panel, else
  > dark-on-light; ~160–180px + a quiet zone)*

*(Again: no places, no "four countries", no city list, no map. Scale is "a real, growing
community" and the crowd of figures — nothing more.)*

## 4. The worldwide-circle / city rail is intentionally ABSENT

This is the headline difference from concepts 1–4 and is deliberate, not an omission to be
"fixed" later:

- There is **no city footer line**, **no `RISHIKESH · UBUD · LONDON · BERLIN · TULUM`**, **no
  "four countries", no map, no place pins, no horizon-of-dots geography rail** anywhere on
  either side. If a place name appears, the concept has failed.
- Where the other concepts place a geographic spine, this concept places a **human spine**: the
  centred ring of people on both sides is the shared element that makes front and back read as
  one object. Front and back are the *same gathering* in two moods — warm/orange for guests,
  grounded/olive for hosts.
- "Scale" and "reach" are carried **only** by the density of the human marks in the ring and by
  abstract language ("a real, growing community", "your people", "an international community" —
  with international meaning *people from everywhere*, never a list). The reader feels the
  community is large and alive without ever being shown a location.

## 5. Tone of the footer / anchoring line (a community line, not locations)

A single quiet line, centred under the wordmark at the foot of **both** sides — the brand's
human spine in place of the others' city spine. It names belonging, never geography. Inter or
small Lora italic, muted `#8C8C8C`, tracked gently, hairline rule above:

- Primary: **one community, many practices.**
- Alternates in the same register (pick one, keep both sides identical so the line is the
  shared anchor):
  - *practise together.*
  - *find your people.*
  - *you belong here.*
  - *one circle, room for everyone.*

It must read as a soft truth about people, never a claim about places — the quiet counterpart to
the centred gathering above it.

## 7. Photo art-direction + Freepik shotlist

Photography rules apply throughout: real people (not models), diverse bodies and ages, calm
focus and quiet joy, natural window light, warm neutrals matching the palette, generous negative
space, observed-not-staged. No altars/incense/mandalas/sacred-geometry/festival aesthetics, no
guru-on-pedestal, no perfect-pose hero. **And: no signage, no skyline, no landmark, nothing that
identifies a city or country** — the room must be placeless.

### FRONT (guests) — zone 3 image (the gathering)

- **Brief:** One warm, centred editorial photograph of a small, diverse group *gathered* — sat
  loosely in a circle after a session, mid-quiet-laugh, leaning in, sharing tea, no performance.
  The composition should read as a ring with an open, calm centre (shoot slightly from above, or
  leave a gap in the circle that frames cream space). Warm window light, muted tones. The
  *people and the togetherness* are the subject; place is unreadable and irrelevant.
- **Freepik cues:**
  - `"diverse group sitting in circle after yoga class candid natural light"`
  - `"small community gathering warm tones real people laughing unposed"`
  - `"people connecting after meditation session window light documentary"`
- **Avoid in selection:** any recognisable building exterior, city window-view, flags, signage,
  or location-identifying detail.

### BACK (hosts) — zone 3 image (turned toward you)

- **Brief:** A quieter centred frame where a community is gently *oriented toward* one person —
  a facilitator welcomed into a circle, or a teacher arriving as a warm group turns to greet
  them. Olive-leaning grade. The feeling is *being found and received*, not performing. Leaves
  the open centre / lower area for the four value lines.
- **Freepik cues:**
  - `"small group welcoming teacher into circle warm natural light candid"`
  - `"facilitator greeted by diverse community real people documentary"`
  - `"people turning toward instructor calm studio warm neutral tones"`
- **Avoid:** stages, microphones, audience-and-speaker hierarchy, any location markers.

## 8. Duotone illustration direction

Self-contained inline SVG. Abstract / semi-abstract, organic flowing lines, rounded soft
geometry, **no facial features**, gender-neutral silhouettes, lots of breathing room. Optional
very-low-opacity `feTurbulence` grain. No drop shadows, no faux-3D, no mandalas/chakras — the
concentric rings here are a *water-ripple / gathering* motif, never a chakra or sacred-geometry
device.

### FRONT (guests) — **cream `#F9F6EF` + orange `#C8702A`** (warm, inviting)

A **centred ring of ~10–14 abstract figures** — simple rounded bodies, no faces, holding the
circle like people gathered round a low fire. They vary in size and lean slightly toward the
centre (belonging, attention turned inward to one another). From behind them, **3–4 hairline
orange concentric arcs ripple outward** — the community growing from the middle. At the centre,
a calm **open cream pool** with one faint, lighter figure-mark just stepping in: *room for one
more — you.* Warm, open, unhurried. The density of figures *is* the "growing community" — no
dots-as-cities, no horizon.

### BACK (hosts) — **cream `#F9F6EF` + olive `#6B6E45`** (grounded, credible)

The **same centred ring**, olive-graded — but the figures are subtly **turned outward / toward
one figure arriving** at the open centre, so the host reads as *received by the community*.
Thin olive concentric rings ripple out (the reach that is already there, waiting). A few small
olive nodes where figures link arm-to-arm suggest *teachers linked to their places* (props c) —
expressed as human connection, never as map points. Stillness and welcome rather than spread.
The same ripple motif ties back to the front so the two sides are one gathering.

## 9. Colour emphasis

- **FRONT (guests) — warm / orange-forward.** Cream page (`#F9F6EF`), ink text (`#1D1D21`)
  warming to orange (`#C8702A`) on the hero's second clause, the accent rule, the CTA, and (in
  duotone) the ring's ripples. Warmth = invitation; this side should feel like being welcomed
  into a circle.
- **BACK (hosts) — grounded / olive-forward.** Cream page, deeper ink (`#1D1D21` / `#12130C`),
  olive (`#6B6E45`) carried by the accent rule, the value markers, the CTA, and the duotone
  ripples. Credible, steady — the reassurance that a real community is already turning toward
  the host.
- Both sides desaturated, no pure black, no neon. The two read as one system in two moods,
  joined by the centred gathering and the single community footer line.
