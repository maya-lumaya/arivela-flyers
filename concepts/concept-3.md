# Concept #3 — "Your Practice Has a Home"

> Territory in one line: **the host toolkit and the practitioner arc made tangible, calm and warm** — a profile that holds your education, places you can add and link to the teachers who hold space there, and (soon) your online offerings, all shown quietly as one home you grow into over time.

---

## 1. Positioning

**The one idea:** ARIVELA isn't a listing wall — it's *a home for a practice*. For a guest, that means you don't just book a class; you find the experience **and** the real person and place behind it, and you can keep coming back as you grow. For a host, that means your teaching life finally has one address: your profile (with your education and lineage), your places, the teachers you hold space with, and — soon — your online offerings. The flyer shows the *system* gently, as a quiet rhythm of cards, so the reader feels "this holds everything" without being sold a dashboard.

**Why this is distinct from the other two concepts:**
- It is **not** the place-led community concept. That concept leads with the five cities and the feeling of a map; here the worldwide circle is the *footer spine*, present but supporting. Our hero is the **arc of growing into a practice** and the **one-home toolkit** — depth over breadth.
- It is **not** the editorial-trust / photography-hero concept. That concept lets one big image and a single line carry trust. Here we deliberately let **structure carry the meaning**: a gentle stepped card rhythm that makes the system legible at a glance. The trust comes from *seeing how it fits together*, not from one beautiful frame.
- Where a "product-system" concept might drift corporate (feature grids, dashboard chrome, checkmarks), this concept keeps every part **warm and human**: cards breathe, language stays a quiet invitation, and the structure feels like shelves in a calm room, not a settings page.

**The feeling we own:** *grounded, generous, quietly capable.* The competent friend who has thought of everything you'll need, and lays it out without ever raising their voice.

## 4. Layout skeleton (shared by both treatments)

One skeleton; the photo builder fills the image zone with a styled empty frame + art direction, the duotone builder fills the *same* zone with inline-SVG illustration. Proportions are share-of-height of the 2480px canvas, all content inside the ~118px safe margin.

### FRONT (guests) — top-to-bottom zones
1. **Masthead / wordmark** — `ARIVELA` (Inter 600, uppercase, ~0.4em tracking) with the 2px orange accent rule centered below. Calm, top-left or centered. *(~9%)*
2. **Eyebrow** — `events · retreats · trainings`, small Inter, muted, letter-spaced. *(~4%)*
3. **Hero** — *"your practice has a home"* in large Lora italic; warm cream field. *(~16%)*
4. **Image-or-illustration zone** — the single focal moment: one wide warm frame (photo) / one inline-SVG scene (duotone). One focal moment only. *(~30%)*
5. **Value block** — subhead + supporting line; quiet, two short lines, generous leading. *(~12%)*
6. **CTA + QR** — orange CTA pill `discover what's near you`, QR (~160–180px) inline beside it, `arivela.co` under the QR. *(~12%)*
7. **Footer — the five cities (worldwide-circle spine)** — `rishikesh · ubud · london · berlin · tulum` as a single hairline-separated row above a thin `#E8E4DC` rule; "one community, lived across the world" as the lead-in. *(~9%)*

### BACK (hosts) — top-to-bottom zones
1. **Masthead / wordmark** — `ARIVELA` as above; on the back the accent rule may be olive to signal the grounded mood. *(~9%)*
2. **Eyebrow** — `for hosts`, small Inter, muted. *(~3%)*
3. **Hero** — "your profile, your places, your teachers — *one home.*" in Lora (emphasis word italic). *(~12%)*
4. **Image-or-illustration zone** — *smaller* than the front (the structure is the hero on this side): a calm band, one focal moment. *(~18%)*
5. **Value block — the stepped cards** — the four props as a vertical stack of soft cards with a gentle left-indent step (1→2→3 stepping inward a touch, like a staircase); card (d) is the quiet, muted "coming soon" card, visually lighter. This is the largest zone — it carries the message. *(~32%)*
6. **CTA + QR** — olive-grounded CTA `create your host profile`, QR (~160–180px), `arivela.co`. *(~12%)*
7. **Footer — the five cities** — same spine as the front: `rishikesh · ubud · london · berlin · tulum` above a hairline, so both sides clearly belong to one community. *(~9%)*

**Shared invariants:** identical masthead treatment, identical footer-cities spine, identical CTA+QR geometry, same card corner radius and hairline color — so guests/hosts and photo/duotone all read as one family. The only deliberate difference: front leans warm/orange, back leans grounded/olive; front's focal zone is larger (the experience), back's value block is larger (the toolkit).

## 5. Photo art-direction + Freepik shotlist

Brand photo rules apply throughout: real people (not models), diverse bodies and ages, calm focus and quiet joy, natural window light, warm neutrals matching the palette, lots of negative space, observed-not-staged. No altars / incense / mandalas / sacred-geometry / festival looks, no guru-on-pedestal, no perfect-pose hero.

### FRONT (guests) — image zone (one wide focal frame)
- **Brief:** A warm, unposed moment that says "the real people and places behind it." Two or three people in quiet conversation at the edge of a session — a sunlit studio or a simple retreat terrace — mid-laugh or mid-listen, not posed to camera. Leaves clear room at the upper-left for the italic hero to breathe.
- **Freepik:** `"yoga studio candid natural light conversation"` · `"small retreat group warm tones real people"` · `"people talking after wellness class window light"`

*(Optional secondary — only if the layout wants a small inset to hint at "the place behind it," kept tiny so it stays one focal moment):*
- **Brief:** A quiet establishing detail of a *place* — an empty, sunlit practice room or a terrace with mats rolled, no people, warm neutrals — signalling "this is a real space you can find."
- **Freepik:** `"empty yoga studio morning light minimal"` · `"calm retreat space interior warm neutral"`

### BACK (hosts) — image zone (calm band, smaller)
- **Brief:** A grounded, generous portrait of a host *at home in their practice* — a teacher or facilitator in their own space, unhurried, looking at their work rather than performing to camera (arranging the room, in soft conversation with one guest). Olive-leaning grade. Plenty of air so the stepped cards below own the page.
- **Freepik:** `"yoga teacher own studio candid natural light"` · `"retreat host welcoming guest warm documentary"` · `"facilitator preparing calm space real person"`

## 6. Duotone illustration direction

Self-contained inline SVG, organic flowing lines, rounded soft geometry, no facial features, gender-neutral silhouettes, lots of breathing room, optional very-low-opacity `feTurbulence` grain. No mandalas/chakras, no faux-3D or gloss. Both sides share the line-weight and rounded-geometry language so they read as a pair.

### FRONT (guests) — **cream + orange**
- **Depicts:** the *arc of growing into a practice* rendered as one continuous breath-curve that begins small and opens outward — a single flowing figure mid-movement, and the line that leaves their body becomes a gentle path that arcs up toward a small constellation of place-dots (the five cities), suggesting "step in tonight, grow into it over time, and it connects to a wider community." Warm, inviting, open.
- **Tones:** cream `#F9F6EF` base + orange `#C8702A` line/figure; optional light tint `#FAF3EA` for the path fill.

### BACK (hosts) — **cream + olive**
- **Depicts:** the *one-home* system as a quiet structure: a soft rounded "house/shelter" silhouette built from a few nested organic arcs, holding three calm motifs inside — a centered seated figure (the profile/lineage), two overlapping circles (a place linked to its teachers), and one faint, lighter-tinted shape set slightly apart (the "coming soon" online offering, present but unobtrusive). Grounded, capable, generous.
- **Tones:** cream `#F9F6EF` base + olive `#6B6E45` line/figures; optional olive-light `#F5F6F0` and a muted tint for the "coming soon" motif so it recedes.

---

## 7. Colour emphasis

- **Guests (FRONT) — warm / orange-forward.** Warm cream field (`#FFF6E9` / `#FAF3EA` accents), orange `#C8702A` for the hero accent rule, the CTA pill, and any key marks. Invitation and warmth lead. Olive used only sparingly (a hairline, a place-dot) so the two sides stay a related pair.
- **Hosts (BACK) — grounded / olive-forward.** Page cream `#F9F6EF` base, olive `#6B6E45` for the masthead accent rule, the stepped-card accents, and the illustration. The CTA stays the brand orange `#C8702A` (CTAs are the one place orange always leads, for consistency and contrast against the olive field) — so the only "loud" warm note on the grounded back is the single action we want the host to take.
- **Shared:** same neutrals, same `#E8E4DC` hairline, same `#1D1D21` text — the family bond. The front feels like a warm welcome in; the back feels like a steady, capable home. Same room, two times of day.
