# Concept #1 — "The Worldwide Circle"

> **Big idea / territory:** one community, lived across the world. The hero is not a place
> and not a product — it's *the circle itself*: an international community already living in
> four countries (Rishikesh · Ubud · London · Berlin · Tulum), and growing. Place-led, quietly
> emotional, expansive. For guests: the experiences that truly matter are part of a real
> community you can belong to. For hosts: you are not posting into the void — you become
> findable to people who already moved across these places.

---

## 1. Positioning

**The one idea:** *belonging at world-scale.* ARIVELA is one circle of people, drawn across
five cities and four countries, and you can step inside it. Every other message on the flyer
hangs off that single emotional spine — the worldwide circle — and both sides feel like two
arcs of the same ring.

**Why this is distinct from the other two concepts:**

- It is **not the editorial-trust concept.** That concept would lead with *credibility* —
  faces, testimony, "people you can trust," a quiet magazine authority. Here, trust is a
  *by-product* of belonging, not the headline. The hero is the constellation of places and the
  feeling of a circle, not a portrait or a pull-quote.
- It is **not the product-system concept.** That concept would lead with *what ARIVELA does* —
  profiles, places, links, the mechanism, the marketplace as a tidy system. Here the mechanism
  is almost invisible on the front and only quietly named on the back. We sell the *map of a
  living community*, not the dashboard.
- It is **not a generic place-led travel concept.** A place-led concept celebrates the
  destinations — Bali sunsets, the Ganges, "see the world." Ours deliberately resists the
  postcard. The five cities are not destinations to *visit*; they are proof that *one community
  already lives there.* The emphasis is the line connecting them, not any single place. Warm,
  expansive, belonging — never wanderlust, never bucket-list.

**Distinct feeling in one breath:** the other concepts make you feel *reassured* (trust) or
*oriented* (system). This one makes you feel *less alone* — that something real is happening in
several places at once, and there's a seat for you in it.

## 4. Layout skeleton (shared by BOTH treatments)

A5 portrait, `1748 × 2480 px`, full-bleed cream background, all content inside the ~118px safe
margin. Both the photo builder and the duotone builder follow these exact vertical zones so the
pair reads as one system. The **only** thing that swaps is what fills the "circle zone" — a
styled photo frame, or an inline-SVG illustration.

### FRONT (guests) — top-down, warm/orange-forward

1. **Masthead / wordmark** — *top, ~8% height.* `ARIVELA` (Inter 600, uppercase, ~0.4em
   tracking, `#1D1D21`) with the 2px orange accent rule centered below. Left-aligned or
   centered; calm, small. The eyebrow "A WORLDWIDE CIRCLE" sits just beneath it in orange.
2. **Hero headline zone** — *~20% height.* The Lora-italic hero, large, ranged left, generous
   leading. Subhead in Inter directly under it. This is the one focal *verbal* moment.
3. **The circle zone (image-or-illustration)** — *~34% height — the centre of gravity.* A wide
   horizontal band that carries the worldwide-circle motif: the soft constellation / the line
   tracing the five cities / the understated map. In photo treatment this is a styled frame; in
   duotone it's the inline SVG. The five city names are anchored *as labels on this artwork*
   (small Lora caps with dot-nodes), so the map and the worldwide-circle line read as one thing.
4. **Value / supporting block** — *~12% height.* The single supporting line + the worldwide-
   circle line ("one community, living across four countries, and growing") set as the emotional
   payoff under the artwork. A thin `#E8E4DC` hairline separates it from the artwork above.
5. **CTA + QR** — *~16% height.* Lowercase CTA "discover what's near you →" with the inline QR
   (~150–190px, dark-module SVG on cream) to its right, "arivela.co" beneath the QR.
6. **Footer with the five cities** — *bottom, ~6% height.* A quiet single line restating
   `Rishikesh · Ubud · London · Berlin · Tulum` in muted meta text — the spine echoed at the
   foot of the page so the circle frames the whole side, top eyebrow to bottom footer.

### BACK (hosts) — same skeleton, mirrored mood, olive-forward

1. **Masthead / wordmark** — *top, ~8%.* `ARIVELA` again; eyebrow "JOIN THE CIRCLE AS A HOST" in
   olive beneath. Same construction as front so the two sides are obviously one piece.
2. **Hero headline zone** — *~18%.* The Lora-italic host hero + the anti-marketer intro line.
3. **The circle zone (image-or-illustration)** — *~26%.* The *same* worldwide-circle motif, but
   re-voiced for hosts: the host sits *inside* the circle (a node that lights up / a figure at
   the centre of the converging lines) — "you become findable on this map." Photo frame or
   inline SVG. The five cities labelled here too, smaller, so the spine carries across both sides.
4. **Value block (the four props)** — *~30% height — the centre of gravity on this side.* The
   four value props stacked as four calm rows (label + one line each), generous row spacing,
   thin hairline dividers. Prop (d) "coming soon" is the last, quietest row with a small chip.
5. **CTA + QR** — *~14%.* "create your host profile →" + inline QR (olive/dark-module on cream),
   "arivela.co" beneath.
6. **Footer with the five cities** — *bottom, ~6%.* Same muted `Rishikesh · Ubud · London ·
   Berlin · Tulum` line, closing the ring on the back exactly as on the front.

**Proportions are rough guides** — front is hero-and-artwork heavy (the feeling), back is value-
block heavy (the offer). Both keep the masthead, the circle zone, the CTA+QR, and the five-city
footer in the same positions so a reader flipping the flyer feels the same architecture twice.

## 5. Photo art-direction + Freepik shotlist

Encode the brand photography rules everywhere: **real people, not models**; diverse bodies and
ages; calm focus and quiet joy (no ecstatic clichés, no guru-on-pedestal, no perfect-pose hero);
natural light and warm neutrals matching the palette; lots of negative space; observed, not
staged. **Avoid** altars, incense, mandalas, sacred geometry, festival aesthetics. Each photo
slot prints as an empty styled frame containing its art-direction text + Freepik cue (no stand-in
image).

### FRONT (guests) — the circle zone

**Brief:** A wide, calm, *atmospheric* frame that can sit *behind or beside* the constellation
of city labels — a sense of place and belonging without naming a single landmark. Think a softly-
lit gathering or a wide warm interior with breathing room, warm neutral tones, generous negative
space so the city-node labels and headline can overlay it. Not a destination postcard — a feeling
of "people, somewhere real, together."
- *Freepik:* "diverse group candid natural light warm tones", "people gathered community calm
  documentary", "warm minimal interior soft window light negative space".

*(Optional small inset frame, if the layout wants a human note under the hero):* one unposed
moment — two people in quiet conversation after a session, side light, no eye-contact-to-camera.
- *Freepik:* "two people quiet conversation studio natural light", "yoga studio candid real
  people diverse", "retreat real people relaxed unposed".

### BACK (hosts) — the circle zone

**Brief:** A host *in their element* but understated — a teacher or facilitator in their own
space (a studio, a retreat home, a training room), seen mid-ordinary-moment, not performing.
Warm window light, real space with a little wear, calm competence. Read it as "a real person who
holds space here," not a brand ambassador. Leaves room for the host hero line and the city labels.
- *Freepik:* "yoga teacher own studio natural light candid", "facilitator preparing space warm
  neutral documentary", "wellness host portrait real person calm not posed".

*(Optional small inset frame, near the "add your places" prop):* a quiet detail of a *place* —
an empty studio floor, mats rolled, a retreat porch — warm and lived-in, no people, lots of air.
- *Freepik:* "empty yoga studio warm light minimal", "retreat space interior natural light calm",
  "training room wooden floor soft daylight negative space".

## 6. Duotone illustration direction

Hand-authored inline SVG, two-tone, organic flowing lines, rounded soft geometry, no facial
features, gender-neutral silhouettes, lots of breathing room. Optional very-low-opacity
`feTurbulence` grain. No drop shadows, no faux-3D, no mandalas/chakras. The motif language for
this concept is **community → a constellation of places / overlapping circles / a connecting
line.** Both sides render the *same* constellation skeleton so the pair is unmistakably one set.

### FRONT (guests) — tones: **cream `#F9F6EF` + orange `#C8702A`** (warm, inviting)

An understated world: five soft dot-nodes (Rishikesh, Ubud, London, Berlin, Tulum) placed across
a barely-there, abstracted landmass / horizon band — connected by a single calm, flowing orange
line that loops the five into one continuous *circle* (an open, not-quite-closed ring, leaving
room to grow). At one node, a small abstract human silhouette (a breath-curve figure) stands —
"you, joining the circle." The line is the hero; the map is suggested, never literal — no real
coastlines, just enough geography to feel worldwide. A faint cream tint inside the ring suggests
a held, shared space.

### BACK (hosts) — tones: **cream `#F9F6EF` + olive `#6B6E45`** (grounded, credible)

The *same* five-node constellation, re-centred on the host: the connecting line is olive now, and
all five lines converge inward toward a single node where an abstract figure holds space (a
centered-stillness shape, arms as an open curve). The other four nodes send soft lines *toward*
this host node — "you become findable; the community moves toward you." Same open-ring geometry,
same city positions as the front, so flipping the flyer rotates the same circle from "I join" to
"I am found." A light olive-tint fill inside the ring grounds it.

**Shared skeleton:** identical node coordinates and ring path on both sides; only the accent tone
(orange→olive), the line direction (looping vs. converging), and the figure's role (joiner vs.
host) change. That is what makes the photo pair and the duotone pair each read as one coherent set.

## 7. Colour emphasis

- **Guests (front) — warm / orange-forward.** Page cream `#F9F6EF` base; orange `#C8702A` carries
  the eyebrow, the connecting ring, the CTA, the accent rule under the wordmark. Warm-cream
  `#FFF6E9` may tint the circle zone. Olive appears only as a quiet grounding whisper (a hairline,
  a city-node dot) so the two sides stay a related pair. Mood: warmth, invitation, belonging.
- **Hosts (back) — grounded / olive-forward.** Same cream base; olive `#6B6E45` carries the
  eyebrow, the converging ring, the value-row accents and the CTA. Orange returns as a single
  small warm accent — the lit host node and the CTA arrow — so the host side still feels part of
  the warm circle, just steadier and more credible. Mood: trust, depth, "you belong here too."
- **The justified link:** orange = the *invitation in* (guests), olive = the *ground you stand
  on* (hosts). Each side leads with its own accent but borrows one touch of the other, so the
  worldwide circle reads as a single ring with two arcs rather than two separate flyers.
