# Concept #2 — "People You Can Trust"

> Big idea: The antidote to Instagram-wellness theatre. Credibility, honesty and
> depth shown through *restraint* — a fine-magazine-cover feel where type leads and
> negative space does the talking. The worldwide circle is a quiet credibility
> signal (a real community across four countries), never the loud hero.

---

## 1. Positioning

**One idea:** *trust is shown, not claimed.* This flyer is the calm opposite of the
glossy, ecstatic, before/after wellness feed. It reassures the jargon-wary guest
(Anna), the lineage-curious sceptic (Daniel — "stop selling me, tell me who trained
the teacher"), and the depth-seeker (Sebastian) by doing the most countercultural
thing a wellness brand can do: leave the page mostly empty, speak plainly, and let
the only "proof" be the things that are actually verifiable — real teachers, real
education on the page, real places, a real circle of cities. No transformation
theatre, no promises, no urgency.

**Why it is distinct from the other two concepts:**

- It is **not the place-led community concept.** Here the five cities are a *footnote
  of credibility* — a thin line at the foot of the page — not the hero. The hero is a
  single, quiet editorial sentence. The "where" is reassurance that the circle is real,
  not the subject.
- It is **not a product-system concept.** There are no feature grids, no UI chrome, no
  "how it works" steps, no badges. The host side names the four capabilities, but as
  four plain editorial lines with breathing room, phrased as *reassurances* ("be
  findable, then we leave you alone to teach"), not as a product spec sheet.
- It owns the **fine-magazine-cover** territory: type-led, almost austere, one focal
  moment, a lot of cream. Where an editorial-trust *story* concept might lean on
  pull-quotes, captions and a documentary-spread richness, this concept is more
  reductive and architectural — the restraint *is* the message. The feeling is a
  thoughtful independent magazine you'd actually keep, not a leaflet you'd recycle.

The emotional promise of the whole piece: *you can relax — these are people you can
actually trust, and we're not going to perform at you.*

## 2. Guests side (FRONT) — copy

- **Eyebrow / kicker** (Inter, uppercase, tracked, muted `#8C8C8C`):
  `EVENTS · RETREATS · TRAININGS`

- **Hero headline** *(Lora **italic**, the signature voice)*:
  > *the ones who teach this*
  > *have actually lived it.*

  *(Two lines, ranged left, large. Italic carries the quiet confidence; the line
  rewards a second read rather than shouting on the first.)*

- **Subhead** (Lora roman or Inter, calmer weight):
  > experiences that truly matter — held by people you can trust.

- **One supporting line** (Inter, secondary `#5C5C5C`):
  > every teacher's education and lineage is on their profile, in plain sight.
  > no transformation theatre. just real practice, real people.

- **The worldwide-circle line** (small, Inter, muted — a quiet credibility signal at
  the foot, with the cities tracked and separated by thin middots):
  > one community, lived across four countries —
  > `RISHIKESH · UBUD · LONDON · BERLIN · TULUM`

- **CTA** (lowercase; orange pill or orange text-link + arrow):
  > discover what's near you →
  > **arivela.co**  *(with QR alongside)*

## 3. Hosts side (BACK) — copy

- **Eyebrow / kicker** (Inter, uppercase, tracked, olive-muted):
  `FOR TEACHERS · FACILITATORS · STUDIOS · SCHOOLS`

- **Hero headline** *(Lora **italic** for the hero line; grounded, olive ink)*:
  > *be found by the people*
  > *who'd love what you do.*

  *(Then a short roman descender beneath, Lora roman:* "then we leave you alone to
  teach." *— the line that wins Sebastian and Rainer.)*

- **The four value props** — four plain editorial lines, each a small olive numeral or
  hairline-separated, generous space between. Warm, on-voice, no hype:

  **(a) more guests for your events, retreats & trainings.**
  reach an international community living across four countries, and growing.

  **(b) create your profile — with your education, training and lineage.**
  your credibility is your story. the years you studied, where, and with whom.

  **(c) add your places, and link the teachers who hold space there.**
  studios, retreat centers, the rooms you actually teach in — connected to their people.

  **(d) coming soon: list your online offerings.** *(quiet "soon" note, not hype)*
  a small line set apart — `SOON` in a thin tracked label, no exclamation.

- **CTA** (lowercase; olive-grounded text-link + arrow):
  > create your host profile →
  > **arivela.co**  *(with QR alongside)*

## 4. Layout skeleton (shared by both treatments)

Both the photo builder and the duotone builder follow **this exact skeleton** on each
side — only the "image-or-illustration zone" swaps its contents. Page = `1748 × 2480`,
all content inside the ~118px safe margin, full-bleed cream background.

### FRONT (guests) — top → bottom

| # | Zone | ~Vertical share | Notes |
|---|------|------------------|-------|
| 1 | **Masthead / wordmark** | ~8% (top) | `ARIVELA` Inter 600 uppercase, ~0.4em tracking, centered or ranged left; 2px orange accent rule beneath. Eyebrow `EVENTS · RETREATS · TRAININGS` sits just under it. |
| 2 | **Hero** | ~22% | The Lora-italic hero line, ranged left, large; deep ink `#1D1D21`. This is the single focal sentence. Lots of cream around it. |
| 3 | **Image-or-illustration zone** | ~34% | One calm, wide focal frame (photo) or one self-contained duotone illustration. Soft 6–8px radius, hairline border in the photo case. Bleeds toward the right/edge but text stays in safe margin. |
| 4 | **Value block** | ~16% | Subhead + the supporting "education & lineage in plain sight" line. Quiet, two short lines, generous leading. |
| 5 | **CTA + QR** | ~12% | Lowercase CTA "discover what's near you →", orange accent; inline QR (~160–180px, dark-on-light) to the side; `arivela.co` beneath. |
| 6 | **Footer — the five cities** | ~8% (bottom) | Hairline divider, then the worldwide-circle line: "one community, lived across four countries —" + `RISHIKESH · UBUD · LONDON · BERLIN · TULUM`, tracked + muted. The quiet spine. |

### BACK (hosts) — top → bottom

| # | Zone | ~Vertical share | Notes |
|---|------|------------------|-------|
| 1 | **Masthead / wordmark** | ~8% (top) | `ARIVELA` as front; eyebrow `FOR TEACHERS · FACILITATORS · STUDIOS · SCHOOLS`. Olive accent rule (the back's grounded counterpart to the front's orange). |
| 2 | **Hero** | ~20% | Lora-italic host hero "be found by the people who'd love what you do." + the short roman "then we leave you alone to teach." |
| 3 | **Image-or-illustration zone** | ~24% | Smaller than the front's — the back is more text-forward. One quiet frame/illustration: a teacher's hands / a profile-as-credential motif. |
| 4 | **Value block — the four lines** | ~30% | The defining zone of the back. Four hairline-separated editorial rows (a–d), olive numerals/markers, lots of space; (d) carries the small `SOON` label. |

The two sides are mirror compositions of one grid: same masthead, same CTA+QR
position, same city footer — so front and back read as one object, two moods.

## 5. Photo art-direction + Freepik shotlist

Photography rules apply throughout: real people (not models), diverse bodies and ages,
calm focus and quiet joy, natural window light, warm neutrals matching the palette,
generous negative space, observed-not-staged. No altars/incense/mandalas/sacred-
geometry/festival aesthetics, no guru-on-pedestal, no perfect-pose hero.

### FRONT (guests) — zone 3 image

- **Brief:** One wide, calm editorial photograph that earns trust by being *ordinary
  and real* — a teacher and a guest in quiet conversation after a session, or a small
  group resting, mid-laugh, no performance. Warm window light, muted tones, lots of
  empty wall/floor so the Lora hero can breathe above it. Reads as a documentary still,
  not an ad.
- **Freepik:**
  - `"yoga teacher talking with student candid natural light"`
  - `"small group meditation real people diverse calm studio"`
  - `"retreat conversation warm window light unposed"`

### BACK (hosts) — zone 3 image

- **Brief:** A quieter, more intimate frame that says *credibility and craft* — a
  teacher's hands demonstrating, or a facilitator setting up a calm room, observed from
  the side. Grounded, olive-leaning grade; focus on competence and presence, not face-
  to-camera charisma. Leaves room beside it for the four value lines.
- **Freepik:**
  - `"yoga teacher hands demonstrating posture close up natural light"`
  - `"facilitator preparing calm studio space candid documentary"`
  - `"experienced instructor teaching small group warm neutral tones"`

## 6. Duotone illustration direction

Self-contained inline SVG. Abstract / semi-abstract, organic flowing lines, rounded
soft geometry, **no facial features**, gender-neutral silhouettes, lots of breathing
room. Optional very-low-opacity `feTurbulence` grain. No drop shadows, no faux-3D, no
mandalas/chakras.

### FRONT (guests) — **cream `#F9F6EF` + orange `#C8702A`** (warm, inviting)

Two abstract figures inclined gently toward each other in quiet exchange — a single
continuous breath-curve line that flows from one body into the other, suggesting *trust
and real connection between teacher and guest*. Behind/beneath them, a thin orange arc
of five small dots (the five cities) traces a quiet horizon — the worldwide circle
present but understated. Warm, open, unhurried.

### BACK (hosts) — **cream `#F9F6EF` + olive `#6B6E45`** (grounded, credible)

A single centered figure in a calm, rooted stance (standing/seated, holding space),
rendered as one confident olive line. Around the figure, a few concentric organic
rings — the *profile / credential* motif: layers of education and lineage radiating
quietly outward, and small olive nodes where teachers link to their places. Stillness
and depth rather than reach. The same thin five-dot arc appears small at the foot, tying
back to the front.

## 7. Colour emphasis

- **FRONT (guests) — warm / orange-forward.** Cream page (`#F9F6EF`), ink text
  (`#1D1D21`), and orange (`#C8702A`) carried by the accent rule, the CTA, and (in
  duotone) the illustration's second tone. Warmth = invitation; this side should feel
  like an open door.
- **BACK (hosts) — grounded / olive-forward.** Same cream page, ink text, but the
  accent shifts to olive (`#6B6E45`) on the rule, the numerals/markers, the CTA, and the
  duotone's second tone. Olive = depth, credibility, "we take you seriously and then get
  out of your way."
- Both sides share the identical cream foundation, ink type, hairline dividers
  (`#E8E4DC`) and muted meta (`#8C8C8C`), so they are unmistakably one system in two
  moods. The orange↔olive shift is the *only* thing that signals "demand side / supply
  side" — restraint, again, doing the work.
