ARIVELA

A5 flyer rebrand · concept gallery

Five creative concepts, each in a photo and a duotone treatment — guests on the front, hosts on the back.

A two-sided printed A5 flyer for ARIVELA — the marketplace connecting conscious-living hosts with the guests who attend their events, retreats and trainings, across one community living in four countries.

04

Off the Center

read concept-4.md →

The same ARIVELA brand, hung off-center. A deliberately asymmetric layout language — type ranged hard against one edge, the five areas dropping diagonally down a margin rail (right on the front, left on the back), and big quiet negative space opposite. A modern art-magazine spread where the white space is part of the composition.

Guests · front

find experiences that truly matter — wherever you are.

Hosts · back

05

You Were Never Meant to Practise Alone

read concept-5.md →

Community is the hero — the people, not the places. Geography is removed entirely; a single centred ring of quiet human marks holds the page, with an open cream heart at its core: room for one more. Front and back are the same gathering in two moods — warm/orange for guests, grounded/olive for hosts — joined by people rather than a city rail. The feeling we own is belonging.

Guests · front

you were never meant to practise alone.

Hosts · back

there's a community already looking for what you hold.
