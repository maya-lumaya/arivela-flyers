# ARIVELA — Freepik Shotlist

A shopping checklist for the **photo-treatment** flyers. Every photo slot across the four
concepts is listed here, organised **by concept** and then **by page/side**, with its
art-direction brief and the exact Freepik search phrases to search against. Tick a slot off once
you've licensed an image for it.

The duotone pages need **no photos** — their illustration is inline SVG. This list covers only the
eight `*-photo-*` pages (one guests/front + one hosts/back per concept).

---

## Photography rules (apply to EVERY slot)

These are non-negotiable; encode them in your selection.

- Real **people, not models**; diverse bodies and ages; inclusive of beginners and elders.
- **Calm focus and quiet joy** — no ecstatic clichés, no guru-on-pedestal, no perfect-pose hero.
- **Natural / window light**, warm neutrals matching the palette (`#F9F6EF` cream, `#C8702A`
  orange, `#6B6E45` olive). Guests skew warm; hosts skew grounded/olive-leaning.
- **Observed, not staged** — documentary stills, not ads. Faces rarely to camera.
- Generous **negative space** so the headline / city labels / value lines can overlay or breathe.
- **Avoid:** altars, incense, mandalas, sacred geometry, festival aesthetics; before/after or
  transformation theatre.

## Concept 1 — "The Worldwide Circle"

The photo sits *behind or beside* the constellation of city labels — a sense of place and
belonging without naming a single landmark.

### c1-photo-guests.html — FRONT (guests) · the circle zone

- [ ] **Primary frame.** A wide, calm, atmospheric image that can sit behind the five city
  labels — a softly-lit gathering or a wide warm interior with breathing room, warm neutral
  tones, generous negative space so the city-node labels and headline overlay it. Not a
  destination postcard — a feeling of "people, somewhere real, together."
  - `"diverse group candid natural light warm tones"`
  - `"people gathered community calm documentary"`
  - `"warm minimal interior soft window light negative space"`

- [ ] **Optional inset** (small human note under the hero) — two people in quiet conversation
  after a session, side light, no eye-contact-to-camera.
  - `"two people quiet conversation studio natural light"`
  - `"yoga studio candid real people diverse"`
  - `"retreat real people relaxed unposed"`

### c1-photo-hosts.html — BACK (hosts) · the circle zone

- [ ] **Primary frame.** A host *in their element* but understated — a teacher or facilitator in
  their own space (studio, retreat home, training room), mid-ordinary-moment, not performing.
  Warm window light, a real space with a little wear, calm competence. "A real person who holds
  space here," not a brand ambassador. Leaves room for the host hero line and city labels.
  - `"yoga teacher own studio natural light candid"`
  - `"facilitator preparing space warm neutral documentary"`
  - `"wellness host portrait real person calm not posed"`

- [ ] **Optional inset** (near the "add your places" prop) — a quiet detail of a *place*: an empty
  studio floor, mats rolled, a retreat porch — warm and lived-in, no people, lots of air.
  - `"empty yoga studio warm light minimal"`
  - `"retreat space interior natural light calm"`
  - `"training room wooden floor soft daylight negative space"`

## Concept 2 — "People You Can Trust"

One focal frame per side, earning trust by being ordinary and real. Lots of empty wall/floor so
the Lora hero can breathe; reads as a documentary still, not an ad.

### c2-photo-guests.html — FRONT (guests) · zone 3 image

- [ ] **Focal frame.** One wide, calm editorial photograph — a teacher and a guest in quiet
  conversation after a session, or a small group resting, mid-laugh, no performance. Warm window
  light, muted tones, plenty of empty space above for the headline.
  - `"yoga teacher talking with student candid natural light"`
  - `"small group meditation real people diverse calm studio"`
  - `"retreat conversation warm window light unposed"`

### c2-photo-hosts.html — BACK (hosts) · zone 3 image

- [ ] **Focal frame.** A quieter, more intimate frame that says *credibility and craft* — a
  teacher's hands demonstrating, or a facilitator setting up a calm room, observed from the side.
  Grounded, olive-leaning grade; competence and presence, not face-to-camera charisma. Leaves room
  beside it for the four value lines.
  - `"yoga teacher hands demonstrating posture close up natural light"`
  - `"facilitator preparing calm studio space candid documentary"`
  - `"experienced instructor teaching small group warm neutral tones"`

## Concept 3 — "Your Practice Has a Home"

The front carries one wide warm focal moment; the back stays text-forward with a smaller, calmer
band so the stepped cards own the page.

### c3-photo-guests.html — FRONT (guests) · image zone (one wide focal frame)

- [ ] **Focal frame.** A warm, unposed moment that says "the real people and places behind it" —
  two or three people in quiet conversation at the edge of a session, a sunlit studio or simple
  retreat terrace, mid-laugh or mid-listen, not posed to camera. Leave clear room at the upper-left
  for the italic hero.
  - `"yoga studio candid natural light conversation"`
  - `"small retreat group warm tones real people"`
  - `"people talking after wellness class window light"`

- [ ] **Optional small inset** (hint at "the place behind it"; keep tiny so it stays one focal
  moment) — a quiet establishing detail of a *place*: an empty, sunlit practice room or a terrace
  with mats rolled, no people, warm neutrals.
  - `"empty yoga studio morning light minimal"`
  - `"calm retreat space interior warm neutral"`

### c3-photo-hosts.html — BACK (hosts) · image zone (calm band, smaller)

- [ ] **Calm band.** A grounded, generous portrait of a host *at home in their practice* — a
  teacher or facilitator in their own space, unhurried, looking at their work rather than
  performing to camera (arranging the room, in soft conversation with one guest). Olive-leaning
  grade. Plenty of air so the stepped cards below own the page.
  - `"yoga teacher own studio candid natural light"`
  - `"retreat host welcoming guest warm documentary"`
  - `"facilitator preparing calm space real person"`

## Concept 4 — "Off the Center"

The photo frame is **off-center and bleeds off the outer edge** (left on the front, right on the
back); direct its **negative space toward the open field / axis side** so the headline and the
margin city-rail never fight the image.

### c4-photo-guests.html — FRONT (guests) · left-bleeding off-center frame

- [ ] **Primary frame** (low-left, bleeds off the left edge; negative space falls right). One tall,
  calm, atmospheric image — a single person or small pair in a warm, lived-in interior near a
  window, off to one side, lots of air on the other. "Someone real, somewhere out there, that you
  could belong with." Keep the right half quiet for the open field.
  - `"person by window warm natural light negative space"`
  - `"diverse people candid documentary warm tones minimal"`
  - `"calm interior single figure soft daylight lots of space"`

- [ ] **Optional inset** (tiny human note tucked into the open field) — two people in quiet
  conversation after a session, side light, no eye-contact-to-camera.
  - `"two people quiet conversation studio natural light"`
  - `"yoga studio candid real people diverse unposed"`

### c4-photo-hosts.html — BACK (hosts) · right-bleeding off-center frame

- [ ] **Primary frame** (low-right, bleeds off the right edge; negative space falls left). A host in
  their element, understated — teacher/facilitator in their own studio, retreat home or training
  room, mid-ordinary-moment, not performing. Warm window light, honest wear, calm competence. Keep
  the left portion quiet for the open field + value block.
  - `"yoga teacher own studio natural light candid off center"`
  - `"facilitator preparing space warm neutral documentary negative space"`
  - `"wellness host real person calm not posed minimal interior"`

- [ ] **Optional inset** (near the "add your places" prop, in the open left field) — a quiet detail
  of a place: empty studio floor, mats rolled, a retreat porch; warm, lived-in, lots of air.
  - `"empty yoga studio warm light minimal negative space"`
  - `"retreat space interior natural light calm"`
  - `"training room wooden floor soft daylight"`
